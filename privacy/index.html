Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data Milimetrik Stüdyo ("the tool", "we") uses when a creator connects a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, why, where it is kept and how to remove it.

1. Data we receive

Only after the creator signs in and approves the permissions on the platform's own consent screen:

  • TikTok: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic); follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats); the list of the account's videos with their IDs, titles, durations and view, like, comment and share counts (video.list); the creator information TikTok returns before posting (nickname, username, allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length). Permissions video.upload and video.publish let the tool send the creator's own finished videos to TikTok; they do not give us any data.
  • YouTube: the channel's ID, title and subscriber count, and statistics of the channel's own videos (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).
  • Instagram: the account's ID and username, and insights of the account's own Reels (Meta Graph API).
  • Access tokens issued by each platform so the tool can act for the creator who connected it.

We do not receive or store any data about viewers, followers or other users, and we do not collect passwords.

2. How we use it

  • To publish videos the creator made to the creator's own account.
  • To show which account is connected before publishing.
  • To measure how the creator's own videos perform (views, likes, comments, shares, followers) and plan future videos.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we never sell, rent or share it with third parties.

3. Where it is stored and for how long

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the creator's own computer, in files readable only by that computer's user account. There is no server or database run by us. Statistics are kept as long as the creator keeps the project files; tokens are kept until the creator disconnects the account or they expire.

4. Disconnecting and deleting data

  • TikTok: TikTok app → Profile → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → Milimetrik Stüdyo → Remove access.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram / Meta: Settings → Apps and websites.

Once access is removed the tokens stop working. To delete the locally stored statistics and tokens, delete the project's .env file and statistics files, or ask us via the contact below and we will do it within 30 days.

5. Third-party services

The tool talks only to the official APIs of TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta. Their own policies apply to data on their platforms: TikTok Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy (the tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service), Meta Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the adult creator who runs it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page.

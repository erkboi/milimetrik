Milimetrik Stüdyo

The publishing tool behind @milimetrikfutbol, a Turkish football analysis channel.

We make short, original, data-driven football videos: our own animations, open match data and our own narration. Milimetrik Stüdyo is the tool we use to publish those finished videos and to see how they perform.

What the tool does

  • Connect: the creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the requested permissions.
  • Publish: a finished vertical video and its caption are sent to the creator's own TikTok account (Content Posting API), either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post, using the settings TikTok returns for the creator.
  • Measure: the tool reads view, like, comment and share counts of the videos it published, plus the account's follower count, so we can see which topics work.

What it does not do

  • It does not read other users' data, messages or comments.
  • It does not download, copy or re-post other people's content.
  • It does not sell or share any data. There are no ads and no third-party trackers on this site.
